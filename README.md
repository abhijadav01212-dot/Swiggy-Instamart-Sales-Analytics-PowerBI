# Swiggy Instamart Sales & Performance Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Swiggy Instamart Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, order trends, customer ratings, shop types, shop tiers, product categories, shop sizes, and store opening-year patterns.

The dashboard transforms raw retail data into meaningful business insights through KPI analysis, interactive filters, comparative visualizations, and detailed performance analysis.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze Instamart retail performance and identify key business trends across:

- Sales performance
- Order volume
- Average sales
- Customer ratings
- Product categories
- Shop types
- Shop tiers
- Shop sizes
- City types
- Store opening years

The dashboard is designed to support data-driven decision-making and help identify high-performing categories, store formats, and business segments.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales & Performance Overview

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview using key performance indicators:

- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Total Orders
- Average Rating

These KPIs provide a quick understanding of overall business performance.

---

### 2. Sales by Category

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across different product categories.

Top-performing categories include:

- Fruits and Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Frozen Foods
- Dairy
- Canned
- Baking Goods
- Health and Hygiene
- Meat

This analysis helps identify high-revenue product categories and understand category-level performance.

---

### 3. Order Analysis by Shop Type

Orders are analyzed across different shop types:

- Supermarket Type1
- Supermarket Type2
- Supermarket Type3
- Grocery Store

This comparison helps identify which store formats contribute the highest number of orders.

---

### 4. Shop City Type Analysis

The dashboard compares sales and order performance across different shop city tiers:

- Tier 1
- Tier 2
- Tier 3

This provides insights into geographical and market-level performance.

---

### 5. Shop Size Analysis

Sales performance is analyzed across different shop sizes:

- Small
- Medium
- High

The visualization helps understand how store size influences sales performance.

---

### 6. Customer Rating Analysis

The dashboard evaluates category performance based on ratings and identifies categories with strong customer ratings.

This helps businesses understand customer satisfaction and product-category performance.

---

### 7. Store Opening Year Analysis

The dashboard analyzes store performance based on opening year to identify trends in store establishment and business activity.

Opening years analyzed include:

- 2021
- 2022
- 2023
- 2024

---

## Key Insights

- Total sales analyzed are approximately **1.20M**.
- The dashboard contains approximately **8.52K total orders**.
- Average sales per transaction are approximately **141.01**.
- The overall average customer rating is approximately **3.92**.
- **Fruits and Vegetables** generated the highest sales among the analyzed categories.
- **Snack Foods** also contributed significantly to total sales.
- **Supermarket Type1** recorded the highest order volume among the analyzed shop types.
- Tier-level analysis provides a clear comparison of sales and order performance across different city types.
- Store size and opening-year analysis provides additional context for evaluating retail performance.
